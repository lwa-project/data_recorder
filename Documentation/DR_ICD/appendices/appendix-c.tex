\chapter{Data Formats Reference}
\label{app:data-formats}

\begin{newInVersion}{2.4.1}
This appendix is new in version~2.4.1.  It provides a complete reference of all
data formats supported by DROS2.  The previously supported TBN and TBW formats
have been removed; DRX8, TBT, TBS, and COR formats have been added.
\end{newInVersion}

\section{Format Summary}

All data formats consist of a frame header followed by sample data transmitted
as UDP packets from NDP.  The DR records complete frames (header~+ samples) to
disk.  Table~\ref{tab:format-summary} summarizes the supported formats.

\begin{longtable}{l r r l}
\caption{Data Format Summary}
\label{tab:format-summary} \\
\toprule
\textbf{Format Type} & \textbf{Frame Size (bytes)} & \textbf{Samples/Frame}
    & \textbf{Sample Type} \\
\midrule
\endfirsthead
\endhead
\bottomrule
\endfoot

DRX  & 4128  & 4096 & 4-bit complex (PackedSample4)  \\
DRX8 & 8224  & 4096 & 8-bit complex (PackedSample8)  \\
TBT  & $28 + 256 \times 32 = 8220$ & $256 \times 32$ & 4-bit complex  \\
TBS (4 ch)  & $28 + 256 \times 8 = 2076$   & $256 \times 8$  & 4-bit complex  \\
TBS (8 ch)  & $28 + 256 \times 16 = 4124$  & $256 \times 16$ & 4-bit complex  \\
TBS (12 ch) & $28 + 256 \times 24 = 6172$  & $256 \times 24$ & 4-bit complex  \\
COR  & 2336  & 288  & 64-bit complex (PackedSample64) \\

\end{longtable}

\begin{noteBox}
TBT and TBS frame sizes depend on the number of stands
(\texttt{TBX\_STAND\_COUNT}), which defaults to 256.  The sizes shown above
assume this default value.  TBT uses $16 \times 2 = 32$ samples per stand
(16~time samples, 2~polarizations).  TBS uses $N_\text{chan} \times 2$
samples per stand, where $N_\text{chan}$ is the number of frequency channels
(4, 8, or~12).
\end{noteBox}

\section{DRX Formats}

DRX carries 4-bit complex samples from the digital receiver.  Four streams
are produced per beam (2~tunings $\times$ 2~polarizations).  The seven filter
(decimation) settings listed in Table~\ref{tab:drx-formats} are available, corresponding to sample rates from
250~kHz to 19.6~MHz.

\section{TBT Format}

TBT (Transient Buffer --- Triggered) carries time-domain voltage data for all stands
at a single tuning (Table~\ref{tab:tbt-format}).  Each frame contains data for all stands with 16~time
samples per channel (2~channels per stand, corresponding to 2~polarizations).

\section{TBS Format}

TBS (Transient Buffer --- Streaming) carries channelized voltage data for all stands
(Table~\ref{tab:tbs-formats}).
Three channel configurations are supported (4, 8, and 12~channels per
polarization per stand); the default is 12~channels.
