\chapter{Error Messages}
\label{app:errors}

When a command cannot be executed, the DR returns \texttt{R} in the R-RESPONSE
field with an error message in the R-COMMENT field.
Table~\ref{tab:errors} lists the defined error messages.

\texttt{Operation not permitted}
    & Operations are scheduled or in progress that prevent execution
      of the requested command. \\

\texttt{Invalid Name}
    & The specified format name contains illegal characters. \\

\texttt{Format Already Defined}
    & The specified format name is already in use. \\

\texttt{Invalid Size}
    & The specified UDP packet payload size exceeds the maximum
      (8192~bytes accounting for headers). \\

\texttt{Invalid Rate}
    & The specified data rate exceeds system capabilities. \\

\texttt{Already Up}
    & Internal storage is already online. \\

\texttt{Not Detected}
    & Internal storage was not detected. \\

\texttt{Cannot Start}
    & File system information could not be determined on the
      specified device. \\

\texttt{Already Down}
    & Internal storage is already offline. \\

\texttt{Invalid Storage ID}
    & The specified storage ID does not exist. \\

\texttt{File not found}
    & The specified tag value does not refer to any file on
      internal storage. \\

\texttt{Invalid Filename}
    & The specified filename contains illegal characters. \\

\texttt{Invalid Position}
    & The requested start byte and length exceed the file size. \\

\texttt{Invalid Range}
    & The requested length exceeds response size limitations. \\

\texttt{Not Scheduled}
    & The specified tag does not refer to any scheduled or
      existing recording. \\

\texttt{Already Stopped}
    & The specified tag refers to an already-completed recording. \\

\texttt{Invalid Time}
    & The requested time frame is in the past or too far in the
      future. \\

\texttt{Time Conflict: <Op>}
    & The requested time frame overlaps with a scheduled operation.
      The first conflicting operation is listed in SCHEDULE-ENTRY-\{n\}
      format. \\

\texttt{Unknown Format: <Fmt>}
    & The specified data format is undefined or unusable. \\

\texttt{Insufficient Drive Space}
    & The storage required exceeds the remaining internal storage. \\

\texttt{Component Not Available: <X>}
    & Internal component X is unavailable, unusable, or
      malfunctioning. \\

\texttt{Bad format: bad argument count}
    & The command arguments could not be parsed; wrong number of
      fields supplied. \\

\texttt{Unsupported channel count}
    & The specified FFT length is outside the valid range
      (32--16384). \\

\texttt{Unsupported integration count}
    & The specified integration count is outside the valid range
      (384--24576). \\

\texttt{Unsupported geometry}
    & The product of Transform Length and Integration Count is not
      an integral multiple of the frame sample count. \\

\texttt{Unknown Stokes output type}
    & The specified Stokes product name in the metadata is not
      recognized. \\

\texttt{Unsupported Stokes output type}
    & The specified Stokes product is recognized but not
      supported. \\

\end{longtable}
